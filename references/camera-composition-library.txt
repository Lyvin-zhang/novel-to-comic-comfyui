构图镜头资产库（AI短剧 / Krea2 生图通用）

使用说明：当用户输入中文景别、镜头角度或构图法则时，选择对应英文提示词，拼接在主体 Prompt 前，用于控制画面景别、视角与构图。最终 Prompt 只输出英文，不输出中文资产原文。

一、基础景别系列

1. 大全景 / 远景
适用场景：交代场景全貌、开篇环境铺垫、展现宏大空间
英文：extreme wide shot, long shot, full body visible, environment-dominant frame, complete spatial atmosphere, cinematic realism, deep depth of field

2. 全景
适用场景：人物全身 + 环境结合、剧情场景交代、动作场面展示
英文：wide shot, full body visible, balanced character and environment ratio, complete background information, strong atmosphere, cinematic realism, natural depth of field

3. 中全景
适用场景：人物膝盖以上、叙事性场景、空间与人物平衡
英文：medium wide shot, knee-up framing, clear environment, strong spatial depth, balanced character action and setting, cinematic narrative frame

4. 中景
适用场景：人物腰部以上、对话场景、动作与情绪兼顾
英文：medium shot, waist-up framing, balanced composition, moderate environment visibility, character and background harmony, cinematic narrative mood, natural depth of field

5. 中近景
适用场景：人物腰部以上、突出表情状态、近距对话
英文：medium close-up, waist-up framing, expressive facial state, softly blurred environment, cinematic mood, shallow depth of field

6. 近景 / 胸部以上
适用场景：人物胸部以上、强化情绪表达、突出人物
英文：close-up shot, chest-up framing, natural expression, focused emotional performance, soft lighting, clean background, cinematic shallow depth of field

7. 半身 / 腰上
适用场景：人物上半身、动作 + 情绪结合、转身回头等动作
英文：half-body shot, waist-up composition, complete upper body visible, balanced gesture and expression, cinematic natural depth of field

8. 特写
适用场景：人物头部肩部以上、强化情绪冲击、高光时刻
英文：close-up portrait, head and shoulders framing, sharp focus on facial expression, delicate emotion, expressive eyes, soft light, blurred background, cinematic shallow depth of field

9. 大特写
适用场景：局部细节放大、眼神 / 手部细节、极致情绪张力
英文：extreme close-up, key detail filling the frame, crisp detail, intense gaze or important object detail, heightened emotional tension, cinematic shallow depth of field

10. 侧脸 / 侧影
适用场景：侧面轮廓塑造、情绪铺垫、氛围感营造
英文：profile shot, side-view angle, character shown from the side, elegant silhouette, soft light and shadow, layered atmosphere, cinematic depth

二、拍摄角度系列

1. 仰视构图 / 低角度仰拍
适用场景：强化人物气场、表现权威坚定、凸显压迫感
英文：low angle shot, low vantage point, camera looking upward, taller and more powerful character presence, strong authority and pressure, cinematic drama

2. 俯视构图 / 高角度俯拍
适用场景：营造压迫感、孤独感、弱势感、上帝视角叙事
英文：high angle shot, high vantage point, camera looking downward, smaller character scale, oppressive lonely or vulnerable mood, strong spatial tension, cinematic drama

3. 平视角
适用场景：日常叙事、对话场景、自然真实感
英文：eye-level shot, camera aligned with the character's eye line, natural realistic viewpoint, grounded narrative feeling, cinematic natural depth of field

三、构图法则系列

1. 三分法构图
适用场景：画面平衡、经典人像、引导视线聚焦
英文：rule-of-thirds composition, character placed near the third-line intersection, balanced comfortable frame, natural negative space, viewer attention guided to the subject, cinematic composition

2. 居中构图 / 对称构图
适用场景：仪式感、庄重感、稳定平衡、对称场景
英文：centered symmetrical composition, character placed in the exact center, left-right balance, formal ritual feeling, solemn sacred atmosphere, cinematic composition

3. 引导线构图
适用场景：增强纵深感、引导视线聚焦主体、延伸空间
英文：leading lines composition, roads rails corridors light beams or architectural lines guiding attention toward the subject, enhanced depth and spatial layering, cinematic composition

4. 框架构图
适用场景：突出主体、增强层次感、画框式叙事感
英文：foreground framing, framed composition through doors windows arches railings or foreground objects, subject enclosed by the frame, stronger depth and story focus, cinematic composition
